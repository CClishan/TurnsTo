## Design Context

### Users
Broad, English-first audiences who need a quick answer or enjoy discovering an unexpectedly useful conversion. Chinese is secondary for the creator and future localized content. The first jobs are estimating a pet's human-equivalent age and translating a color into practical CSS-ready formats.

### Brand Personality
TurnsTo is precise, playful, and quietly confident. It should make complex or surprising conversions feel immediate and trustworthy, without pretending that estimates are certainties.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a restrained Geist-derived light interface with a matching dark mode. Borrow Otty's keyboard-aware focus, dense-but-calm utility surfaces, and immediate feedback. Use Fluid Functionalism patterns for meaningful proximity hover, spring-like state changes, copy confirmation, and accessible color selection; avoid decorative motion.

### Design Principles
- Make the answer immediate; show the method and limits beside it.
- Keep tools local, shareable, and useful without an account.
- Use a quiet neutral field with one purposeful blue action accent.
- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, and reduced-motion alternatives.
- Write structured, crawlable content as carefully as the interactive UI.
